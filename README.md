# Openquote Care

**Local-first, AI-assisted records for ongoing counseling work.**

Openquote Care is a desktop app for counselors who keep continuing relationships with the people they support. It keeps subjects, cases, sessions and schedules together, produces the statistics and documents that reporting requires, and suggests candidate classifications from past records — the counselor always makes the final call.

- **Local-first** — records stay on your machine. No account is required, and the core features work offline.
- **Built for the domain** — subjects, cases, sessions, editable code tables and report-ready statistics come built in.
- **Suggestions, not decisions** — AI proposes candidates; people confirm.

> Status: early development. Nothing is released yet.
